# LimoFind

> LimoFind is a United States consumer marketplace and quote-request service for ground
> transportation rentals — limousines, party buses, charter buses, sprinter buses, mini buses and
> shuttle buses — connecting travelers with local operators near them. Quote requests are organized
> by occasion and vehicle class across state and city landing pages. LimoFind publishes no public
> API, SDK, or developer documentation.
